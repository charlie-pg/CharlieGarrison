# Lab 08 - Deal Evidence and Valuation Triangulation

## Peer Policy

My peers should be large public pharmaceutical companies that make most of their money from prescription drugs and have similar exposure to high growth patented medicines. I would prefer companies with diabetes or obesity exposure. I would exclude companies that mainly operate outside pharmaceuticals or have negative annual earnings.

## Rejection Criteria

I would reject a peer if its main business is too different from Lilly, its annual EPS is negative, or its business model makes its P/E difficult to compare with Lilly.

The peer-based value is much higher than the DCF value. The very wide peer range of $472.43 to $2,528.29 also shows that the relative valuation is highly sensitive to peer selection and differences in reported earnings.

Defer. The DCF supports $371.59 per share while the peer P/E approach supports a median of $1,500.36, so the two methods do not provide a consistent valuation signal.

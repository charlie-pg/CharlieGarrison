# Investment committee memo

OpenAI Codex assisted with the repository audit, code, data checks, tests and drafting. The analysis and investment decision are mine. This project is for education and is not investment advice. See the [validation and AI-use record](validation-and-ai-use.md).

**PILOT the decision process in a paper account. Approve 0% funded allocation.** The investor is 25 and saving for retirement over 30 years. The committee is asked to approve a paper pilot and review issuer evidence and the client’s risk tolerance before considering funding.

Use 60% VT / 40% BIL as the strategic benchmark and rebalance it monthly. The mandate requires at least 5% explicit cash. BIL does not count toward that floor. The proposed 15% annualized volatility limit is an illustrative analyst assumption. Lab 13 supplies no numerical client limit. The 6.5% first-year net expected-return hurdle is solely an illustrative modeling assumption.

## Evidence and proposed range

On the retrospective price snapshot ending September 30, 2026, the minimum-volatility reference candidate under the modeled constraints is 50% BIL, 40% VT and 10% cash. Estimated first-year net return is 6.56% at zero modeled trading cost and annualized volatility is 6.02%. Both raw baselines fail the 5% cash floor. The candidate meets the cash rule and proposed volatility limit. Combined company exposure remains **UNVERIFIED**. The full-window benchmark shows an 8.57% annualized gross historical mean and 8.99% annualized volatility.

Across the six paper scenarios the search produced 45–80% BIL, 15–50% VT, 5–20% cash, and 0% IEF and LQD. These are marginal ranges across modeled outcomes. They are not independently selectable weights or a funded allocation recommendation. The same inputs produce a 30 percentage-point weight shift in the short-window scenario. The 6.5% modeled hurdle is a legacy scenario assumption and the search can add equity exposure after a negative return shock to clear it. The official ALFRED vintage shows the last available DGS10 observation at 5.26% on September 29. This informs Treasury duration monitoring. See the [executed scenarios](executed-output.md).

## Monitoring and reversal

At every decision or rebalance, and during monthly paper review, check cash, total exposures across funds, risk versus the proposed 15% cap and the age of the Treasury signal. Stop funding if explicit cash is below 5%, known exposure to a company exceeds 10%, issuer coverage is incomplete, the proposed volatility limit is exceeded, a required scenario has no feasible result, or the DGS10 vintage is unavailable or older than seven calendar days at the decision date. The seven-day threshold is an illustrative paper-pilot rule. Also review material changes in weights or costs before reconsidering deployment.

Return to the committee with confirmed client risk tolerance, dated holdings and issuer mappings, and scenarios that clear all gates. The 36-month sensitivity and the unexplained historical Lab 15 numbers both warrant continued paper review.
